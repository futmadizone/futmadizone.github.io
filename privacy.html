Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FUT MADI ZONE (FMZ) and its private publishing tool FMZ Studio OS (the "App") handle information.

1. Who uses the App

The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the FUT MADI ZONE channels to prepare, schedule and publish content on the owner's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no user sign-up.

2. Information the App accesses

When the owner connects an account, the App may access, through the official APIs of each platform:

  • YouTube (Google): channel information, video statistics, and uploading videos to the owner's channel.
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram): the owner's Page and professional Instagram account information, post statistics, and publishing posts.
  • TikTok: basic profile information, video statistics, and publishing videos to the owner's account.

The App only accesses the owner's own accounts. It does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers, followers or other users.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to publish and schedule the owner's own content, to show the owner statistics about their own content, and to help plan future content.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer or private server. Media files to be published are hosted on a media hosting service so that platforms can fetch them. Tokens are never shared with third parties.

5. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share information with third parties, except with the platforms themselves (Google/YouTube, Meta, TikTok) when publishing content at the owner's request.

6. Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Data deletion

The owner can disconnect any account at any time, which deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked from each platform's security settings.
